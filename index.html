IRWR — an independent registry with global reach

IRWR documents and verifies extraordinary achievement submitted through GBR. We've built a review network spanning dozens of countries to keep the process fast and credible.

Every accepted claim is checked against the same evidentiary standard — witnesses, measurement, and a documented trail — before it reaches the public archive.

Our Goals

  • Set a consistent, transparent evidentiary standard
  • Expand the independent reviewer network
  • Build partnerships with national record bodies
  • Keep the public archive free and searchable
  • Support record holders through the full process
