IRWR is the certifying registry behind every recognized claim

IRWR reviews and catalogs record claims from around the world. Every claim moves through the same evidentiary standard, regardless of category or country of origin.

Once a claim is accepted, it receives a permanent IRWR index number and enters the public archive — searchable, verifiable, and never removed.

Our Goals

  • Apply one consistent evidentiary standard to every category
  • Grow an independent panel of category reviewers
  • Keep the public archive free, searchable, and permanent
  • Issue a verifiable IRWR ID for every accepted claim
  • Support claimants from first submission through certification
